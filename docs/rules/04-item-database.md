# UNDERKEEP — Item Database
*Companion to the Core Rule Set, Bestiary, and Traps, Locks & Treasure. Every weapon, armor, charm, consumable, tool, and valuable in the game, plus identification, curses, magic properties, loot generation, and shop stock.*

---

## 1. Item Basics

### Equipment Slots

The hero has four equipment slots:

| Slot | Holds |
|---|---|
| **Weapon** | One weapon. Two-handed weapons also block the Off-Hand slot |
| **Off-Hand** | A shield, or nothing |
| **Armor** | One suit of armor |
| **Charm** | One ring, amulet, or trinket |

Equipped items **don't** use inventory slots.

### Inventory

The hero carries **10 + (MIG mod × 2)** slots. Gold takes no space.

| Item | Slots |
|---|---|
| One-handed weapon, charm, tool | 1 |
| Two-handed weapon, shield, light armor | 2 |
| Tower shield | 3 |
| Heavy armor | 4 |
| Potions, scrolls, bombs, torches, rations, herbs | Stack up to **5** per slot |
| Gemstones and valuables | Stack up to **10** per slot |

The **Town Stash** stores up to 50 slots of items safely between trips.

### Quick Slots

Up to **4 consumables** can be pinned to the combat bar for one-tap use. They still count as inventory.

### Rarity

| Rarity | Color | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Common | White | Basic gear and supplies |
| Uncommon | Green | +1 gear, most scrolls, useful potions and charms |
| Rare | Blue | +2 and +3 gear, magic properties, powerful charms and potions |
| Unique | Gold | Named items. Each exists **once per game** |
| Cursed | Red (only once revealed) | Negative bonus and a curse |

### Buying and Selling

- Shops sell at the listed price and buy at **50%**.
- **Gemstones and valuables** sell at **100%**.
- **Unidentified items** sell as if they were plain (+0) versions, so identifying first usually pays.
- Luck lowers shop prices by 5% per positive LCK mod. The Merchant's Seal charm also helps.

### No Durability

Items don't wear out. The only exceptions are specific events: slime **corrosion** (−1 damage, repaired at the Shop for 10 gp), lockpicks and poles breaking, and potions or scrolls destroyed by traps or water. This keeps inventory management light on a phone.

### No Ammunition

Bows, slings, and crossbows have unlimited ammunition. Thrown weapons are recovered after each combat.

## 5. Identification

Magic gear, most potions, and all scrolls are found **unidentified**.

### How Unknown Items Appear

- **Gear** shows its base type only: *"Unknown Long Sword"* or *"Plain Ring."*
- **Potions** have a random appearance that's reshuffled every game: *Murky, Bubbling, Silver, Smoky, Crimson, Amber, Violet, Glowing, Milky, Oily, Fizzy, Black.*
- **Scrolls** have random nonsense titles, also reshuffled every game (*"Scroll labeled VORN ESKEL"*).
- **Healing Potions and Antidotes** are always recognized. They're sold in town from the start.

Once one item of a potion or scroll type is identified, every item of that type is recognized for the rest of the game.

### Ways to Identify

| Method | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Sage** (town) | 20 gp per item | Always works |
| **Lore** skill | Free | Items are identified when picked up |
| **Scroll of Identify** | 40 gp | Identifies every item in your pack |
| **Buying** | — | Anything bought in a shop is identified |
| **Drinking** a potion | Risky | Learns the type, and you get its effect, good or bad |
| **Reading** a scroll | Risky | Learns the type and casts it. A combat spell read outside combat is wasted |
| **Equipping** gear | Risky | The bonus shows after one combat. A property shows when it first triggers. **A curse reveals itself immediately**, and the item binds to you |
| **Wearing** a charm | Risky | Its effect is revealed after 100 steps, or when it first triggers |

## 6. Cursed Items

Unidentified magic weapons, armor, shields, and charms have a chance to be cursed: **5% on floors 1–2, 10% on floors 3+.**

- A cursed weapon, armor, or shield has a **−1 or −2** bonus instead of a positive one.
- Every cursed item carries **one curse** from the table below.
- **Half** of cursed items also carry a normal property, which makes them tempting to keep.
- A cursed item **can't be unequipped** once worn.
- **Removing a curse:** a Scroll of Remove Curse (the item can then be unequipped and sold), or the Temple for **50 gp × the floor it was found on**, which destroys the item.

| d8 | Curse | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Leaden | −2 initiative |
| 2 | Hungering | Camping uses 2 rations |
| 3 | Beacon | Wandering monsters appear on a 1–2 instead of a 1 |
| 4 | Clumsy | −1 DEF |
| 5 | Gloom | Torches and lantern oil burn twice as fast |
| 6 | Draining | −3 max FP |
| 7 | Bloodthirsty | +2 damage, but you can't Defend or Flee |
| 8 | Unlucky | −2 to your LCK mod |

### Gear Details

1. **Base type:** pick randomly from the weapon or armor table, weighted toward items the hero can use (one reroll if the hero doesn't meet the requirement).
2. **Bonus:** Uncommon is +1. Rare is +2; on floors 8+, roll d6 and a 5–6 makes it +3.
3. **Property:** Rare gear has a **50%** chance of a property (entries that name a property always have one).
4. **Unidentified:** all magic gear starts unidentified.
5. **Curse check:** 5% on floors 1–2, 10% on floors 3+.

## 16. Mobile Interface

- **Inventory screen:** a grid of slots with rarity-colored borders and a slot counter (*"12 / 14"*). Unidentified items show a **?** badge; cursed items show red once revealed.
- **Compare view:** tapping gear shows a side-by-side with the equipped item and green/red arrows for each changed stat.
- **Quick slots:** four item buttons on the combat bar, set by long-pressing an item.
- **Auto-sort** and a **"Sell all junk"** button in the Shop (sells monster parts and valuables the player has marked).
- **Loot popup:** after combat, a card lists what dropped with **Take all** and per-item Take buttons. If the pack is full, the popup offers to drop or swap something.
